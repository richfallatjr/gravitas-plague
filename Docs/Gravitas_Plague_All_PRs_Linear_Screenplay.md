# GRAVITAS PLAGUE

## All Authored PRs — Linear Script-Reading Draft

This draft places every production PR cue in gameplay playback order and formats the material for a table read. Dialogue is verbatim from the production transcript source. Short action lines are editorial bridges, not new canon dialogue.

The numbered story beats run from PR 01 through PR 50. Robot beats 17 and 18 expand into five lettered branch cues, producing 53 total production PR cues in the document.

Generated PromptVoice and ConversationVoice responses are not printed because their final wording varies at runtime. The Prologue Script Voice headline is also not a PR; its position is marked only so the two surrounding Big Mike PRs can be evaluated in the correct order.

The post–Mrs. Dempsey Prologue devices can be played in more than one order. This reading follows the existing authored linear order: Crank Radio, Walkie, Ham Receiver, Dad Photo. The Chapter 1 Robot encounter branches; all possible Robot PRs appear in their chronological branch positions. Chapter 2 is presented in gameplay order, including its flashback material.

Three Rich cues in Chapter 1 are audio-only in their production descriptors. Their existing local MP3 transcriptions are included and labeled. The final Angel cue has no author-approved transcript in the repository and is therefore represented as an audio-only beat rather than invented dialogue.

## PROLOGUE — “THEY ARE NOT HUMAN. THEY ARE MONSTERS.”

### INT. RICH'S CABIN — NIGHT

*The county has lost power. A PLAY icon waits above the walkie-talkie. Rich presses it. Static breaks the silence.*

### PR 01 — WALKIE: FIRST CONTACT

BIG MIKE (V.O., WALKIE)

Hey Rich you there? Haven't heard from you in a minute. Do you copy? Over......Rich this is Big Mike! Do you Copy?! Over.

*A generated Big Mike continuation asks Rich to answer. The player responds. Rich keys the walkie.*

### PR 02 — WALKIE: RICH ANSWERS

RICH

Yeah, Mike, I’m here. Power died about ten minutes ago. Front deadbolt’s set, back latch too. The crank radio is pulling pieces of county traffic and one clean Gravitas message, which somehow makes me trust it less. Backup battery’s at forty-two percent. I heard a hard scrape outside, then something hit a car, but I stayed off the glass. I’m at the bench now, checking the ham set. Give me a second—the receive meter just moved.

*Rich's generated continuation asks Mike what he personally saw. Mike answers automatically.*

### PR 03 — WALKIE: MRS. DEMPSEY IN THE STREET

BIG MIKE (V.O., WALKIE)

Copy. Keep the transmitter cold. I saw Mrs. Dempsey in the street, barefoot, staring at your house. A car alarm chirped two doors down and she turned toward it before the second beep. Then she walked face-first into a parked sedan hard enough to open her cheek and kept moving. That part I saw myself. Now something just hit my service door, so I’m stepping away from the glass. Stay dark, stay locked, and don’t key up again yet.

*Mrs. Dempsey reaches Rich's door. The battle music starts. Five seconds into the score, Rich's voice cuts through the room.*

### PR 04 — BATTLE: MRS. DEMPSEY

RICH

Mrs. Dempsey. You need to back up. Don’t make me do this to you. You are not well. Mrs. Dempsey—look at me. It’s Rich. You know me. Jesus—Mike, she’s not stopping. She came through the doorway and she’s coming straight at me. Ma’am, get back! Get—off me! Damn it! Mike, she’s bleeding, and she doesn’t even feel it. I’m moving toward the kitchen. Mrs. Dempsey, last warning. I do not want to hurt you. Back up. Back up now!

*Rich survives. The house becomes quiet again. In this reading, he turns to the crank radio first.*

### PR 05 — CRANK RADIO: EMERGENCY BROADCAST

BROADCASTER (V.O., RADIO)

This is an automated emergency broadcast.

Please stay in your homes. Lock all doors and windows. Do not approach individuals displaying cloudy eyes, loss of speech, abnormal posture, or unprovoked aggression.

The pathogen currently designated ATNV-15 affects rapid fear-response pathways in the mammalian brain. Infected hosts may appear confused, injured, or familiar.

Do not attempt physical contact. Do not attempt verbal de-escalation at close range. Recognition does not indicate recovery.

If a host has entered your residence, create distance. Avoid bodily fluid exposure. Remain calm. Scan your surroundings. Wait for containment instructions.

This message will repeat.

Stay indoors. Stay quiet. Stay away from the infected.

*The official voice continues in generated form, refusing to claim a verified cause or cure. Rich returns to the walkie.*

### PR 06 — WALKIE: AFTER MRS. DEMPSEY

RICH

Mike... I don't even know what to think anymore. Everything happened so fast. I keep telling myself I did what I had to do, but saying it doesn't make it sit any better. The house is quiet again--for the moment--and I'm standing here trying to figure out what comes next. Police services have been down for weeks. Nobody's coming to sort this out for me. I need a minute. Go ahead. What'd you find?

### PR 07 — WALKIE: BIG MIKE'S LEAD-IN

BIG MIKE (V.O., WALKIE)

Rich... you gotta hear this stuff. I picked something up on the radio, but I'm not saying much over an open channel. Whoever was talking sounded scared enough that I'm inclined to believe 'em. Could be nothing... could be everything. Just listen first, then we'll sort out what matters and what doesn't.

*NON-PR INTERLUDE: Big Mike reads the exact authored “THE GRAVITAS PLAGUE SPREADS” Script Voice report. The report is mechanically rendered, but it is not a prerecorded cue.*

### PR 08 — WALKIE: BIG MIKE CHECKS ON RICH

BIG MIKE (V.O., WALKIE)

Rich, you still with me? I heard enough to know something happened, but keep the details off this channel. Just tell me you’re standing and the house is secure. I’ve been checking the regular bands, and they’re getting worse—dead air, crossed signals, people talking over each other. That old ham setup of yours might be the only thing with enough reach. See if it powers up. Check the antenna. Don’t transmit yet. Just let me know if it’s alive.

*Rich powers the old ham receiver. Tuning static resolves into a distant human voice.*

### PR 09 — HAM RECEIVER: A VOICE FROM GEORGIA

CATEYE81 (V.O., HAM RADIO)

Any station monitoring, this is Cat Eye Eight-One, transmitting from Chatham County, Georgia. I haven’t heard a voice I could verify in three days. Savannah’s gone quiet, and most of what’s left on the bands is static or folks repeating rumors. I’ve been sweeping north, west, and down the coast for emergency beacons, road reports, or word of safe passage through the South. I’m sheltered for now, but power and supplies won’t last forever. If anybody copies, answer with a handle and general region only—no exact locations. Reckon I just need to know somebody else is still listening.

### PR 10 — HAM RECEIVER: RICH ANSWERS

RICH

Cat Eye Eight-One, this is Rich. I copy you from the Pennsylvania region. My handle’s Rich—exact location stays off the air for now. I heard a report about a Gravitas beacon, something that can call an antigen drone and get doses out to isolated stations. Have you heard anything you trust? If it’s real, give me the band, the exact dial frequency, and what I’m listening for. My set’s old, so say it slow. Over.

### PR 11 — HAM RECEIVER: THE GRAVITAS BEACON

CATEYE81 (V.O., HAM RADIO)

Rich, Cat Eye Eight-One. Copy Pennsylvania. Yeah—the Gravitas relay is real. I’ve heard the identifier myself, and I copied one station confirming an antigen drop. Go to sixty meters, Channel Two. Set your dial to five point three four six five megahertz, upper sideband. That’s 5.3465 USB. Listen for three clipped data bursts, about a minute apart. Don’t key over the first pass. Once the identifier repeats clean, move to Channel One—5.3305 USB—and check in with your handle and six-character grid square, not a street address. They’ll confirm the request there. If they accept it, Gravitas sends the antigen package by drone and homes it on your carrier. You copy?

*Later, Rich studies an old wedding photograph of himself and Dad.*

### PR 12 — DAD PHOTO: WHAT RICH INHERITED

RICH

That’s Dad and me at a wedding, about twenty years ago. I barely recognize that version of myself. He was still around then, and the world still made sense. Dad went missing early in the outbreak. No call I could trust, no message, nothing that told me where he went. I keep looking at this picture like it’s gonna give me an answer. He always knew how to take a broken system apart and find the one thing everybody else missed. A lot of what’s keeping me alive came from him. I just don’t know if he’s still out there.

*FADE TO BLACK.*

## CHAPTER 1 — “DAD?”

### INT. RICH'S CABIN — NIGHT

*The room returns. Rich keys the walkie with the first good news anyone has had in days.*

RICH

Big Mike, I found it. The Gravitas beacon is real. A woman on the ham bands—Cat Eye Eight-One—walked me through the relay. Sixty meters, Channel Two: five point three four six five megahertz, upper sideband. Then Channel One: five point three three zero five, upper sideband. I sent my delivery grid—Foxtrot November One Zero Golf Mike. They repeated my handle, gave me a confirmation code, and accepted a request for one antigen pack. They said a drone would home in on my carrier when it gets close. Write all that down, Mike. This might actually work.

### PR 14 — WALKIE: SOMEONE IS COMING

BIG MIKE (V.O., WALKIE)

Rich, I copy. Five point three four six five, then five point three three zero five—both upper sideband. Grid Foxtrot November One Zero Golf Mike. I got it. That is damn good news, but now you gotta hear this. I saw a man moving toward your house. Decent size—lot bigger than Mrs. Dempsey. I only caught a quick look, so I cannot tell if he is infected or just desperate. Lock down what you can. Keep something between you and that door, and stay off the windows. I’m tuning those beacon channels now. You stay sharp.

*A large figure reaches the side window. It is Dad. He stands at the glass, silent, then starts to walk away.*

### PR 15 — WINDOW: DAD RETURNS

Dad? Dad? Dad, is that you? Look at me. Come around to the front door. Come on. How did you get here? Where have you been? Dad? Are you okay? Can you hear me? Say something. Dad, don't walk away.

GRAVITAS ROBOT

Please remain still. This unit is operating on behalf of the Gravitas Corporation. A brief diagnostic scan is required. Face forward, and keep your head as still as possible.

#### ROBOT BRANCH A — FULL COMPLIANCE

GRAVITAS ROBOT

Diagnostic scan complete. No late-stage behavioral markers detected. One Gravitas antigen pack has been authorized. Antigen is effective only during early-stage infection. It may temporarily suppress progression. It is not a cure. Payload transfer complete.

### PR 18A — ROBOT: EXIT CONFIRMATION

GRAVITAS ROBOT

Delivery confirmed. Remain indoors and monitor authorized frequencies for further instructions. This unit is returning to relay operations.

#### ROBOT BRANCH B — MOVEMENT, THEN RESTORED COMPLIANCE

### PR 17B — ROBOT: COMPLIANCE WARNING

GRAVITAS ROBOT

Movement detected. This is your final compliance warning. Continued resistance will be interpreted as late-stage infection and loss of behavioral control. Remain still, or defensive measures will be authorized.

### PR 18B — ROBOT: COMPLIANCE RESTORED

GRAVITAS ROBOT

Compliance restored. Scanning in progress. Keep your head facing forward until confirmation.

*The encounter rejoins PR 17A, SUCCESSFUL SCAN, followed by PR 18A, EXIT CONFIRMATION.*

#### ROBOT BRANCH C — UNIT DESTROYED

*Rich destroys the Robot. Its death animation completes before the payload protocol runs.*

### PR 17C — ROBOT: EMERGENCY PAYLOAD RELEASE

GRAVITAS ROBOT

Critical unit failure. Delivery protocol interrupted. Emergency payload release authorized. One antigen pack has been released for recovery. Antigen is intended for early-stage infection only.

*All branches leave Rich with one antigen pack containing four cartridges. He returns to the photograph.*

### PR 19 — DAD PHOTO: FOUR CHANCES

RICH

That picture was twenty years ago. Dad looks like he knew exactly where he belonged. I keep looking at his face in it, then thinking about the face I saw at that window. He didn’t wander past the house. He came right to the glass and stopped. For a second, I swear he knew me. Now I’ve got four antigen cartridges sitting on that cart. Four chances. I don’t know how long he’s been sick, or whether early stage is already behind him, but I can’t decide he’s gone. Not after he found his way home.

*Rich tells Mike who was at the window.*

### PR 20 — WALKIE: IT WAS DAD

RICH

Mike, the man at the window was Dad. I saw his face. He came straight to the house and stood there looking in like he remembered it. I called to him, told him to come to the front door, but he just watched me, then turned back toward the trees. He looked sick—I know that—but he remembered this place. He has to still be in there somewhere. I’ve got four antigen cartridges, and one of them is for him. Watch the roads while I figure out how to bring him back without getting either of us killed.

### PR 21 — WALKIE: BIG MIKE REFUSES THE SUICIDE MISSION

BIG MIKE (V.O., WALKIE)

Rich, slow down and listen. I believe you saw your father. I also believe those woods could be crawling with infected. One familiar face does not make that route safe. Mark the direction he went, lock the house down, and keep those cartridges out of sight. Give me time to watch the roads and work the radio. We can find a way to draw him back to the house without you wandering out there blind. You do not go chasing him alone. Not tonight. I know what he means to you, but I need you alive long enough to help him.

*Rich calls CatEye81 for anything she knows about the treatment window.*

### PR 22 — HAM RECEIVER: WHAT COUNTS AS EARLY STAGE?

RICH

Cat Eye Eight-One, this is Rich. Your relay information worked. A Gravitas unit reached my location and delivered an antigen pack. The drop was real. The instructions say early-stage infection only, but they don’t tell me what early stage actually looks like. I have someone nearby who can still walk, still find familiar places, maybe even recognize a voice. I need to know what signs mean there’s still time, and what happens if the dose is given too late. I want to use this to help people, not just lock it away. What have you heard that you trust? Over.

### PR 23 — HAM RECEIVER: CATEYE81'S WARNING

CATEYE81 (V.O., HAM RADIO)

Rich, Lord, I am glad that relay found you. But listen close: treat that antigen like the last medicine on earth, because it may be. Every report I copied said early stage—confusion, fever, still recognizing people and places. Once the eyes cloud and speech goes, nobody I trust could promise anything. Look after yourself first, and keep the package off the air. Before I went underground, I saw infected pull a young couple down outside Savannah and start feeding. They moved together like a pack. I know what the doctors call this, but some nights it feels like Hell opened its gates and gave the abominations free reign.

*Dad returns through the front door. The battle music begins. At thirty seconds, Rich is still trying to use the antigen instead of hurting him.*

### PR 24 — DAD BATTLE: ONE MORE CHANCE

*Local transcription of the authored MP3; the production descriptor contains no transcript.*

RICH

No, Dad. Wait, wait. I have the antigen right here. Look at me. It's me. Easy, Dad. Easy. You're hurting me. Stop. I don't want to fight you. Just hold still and let me help you. Dad, please. I could still save you. Don't make me do this. Dad.

*The fight turns lethal. With Dad one accepted hit from death, Rich breaks.*

### PR 25 — DAD BATTLE: RICH BREAKS

*Local transcription of the authored MP3; the production descriptor contains no transcript.*

RICH

Dad. Dad, you made me do this to you. Why wouldn't you just stop? Why did you let this happen? I had the antigen. I had it right here. I thought I could save you, Dad. Are you okay? Can you hear me? Please, say something. Dad? Dad, I'm sorry. I'm so sorry.

*Dad dies. Rich buries him near the creek and returns to the photograph.*

### PR 26 — DAD PHOTO: EULOGY

RICH

Dad… I put you down near the creek. It was the only place that felt right. You always liked going down there. Remember when we used to fish before the sun came up? You'd tell me I was talking too loud and scaring everything away.

I wanted so badly to use the antigen on you. I kept thinking there had to be one more chance—that if I could just get close enough, I could bring you back. But it was too late. I know that now. And after what happened in that room… I'm going to need one of those cartridges for myself.

I'm sorry, Dad. I wish I could've saved us both.

*FADE TO BLACK.*

## CHAPTER 2 — “THE NIGHT THE LIGHTS WENT OUT”

### INT. RICH'S CABIN — NIGHT
