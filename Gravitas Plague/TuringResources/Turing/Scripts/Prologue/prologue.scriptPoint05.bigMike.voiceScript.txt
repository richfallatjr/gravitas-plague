Federal Public Health Service advisory. Residents should remain indoors and avoid close contact with anyone showing sudden confusion, aggression, loss of coordination, or an inability to respond normally to injury. Local police, fire, and medical services may be delayed or unavailable. Secure doors and windows. Keep battery-powered radios tuned to verified public-safety frequencies. Do not travel unless authorities order an evacuation. Do not attempt to restrain an affected person alone. If confronted, create distance, move behind a locked barrier, and wait for further instructions.
